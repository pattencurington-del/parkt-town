About ParkT-Town

ParkT-Town is a proposed smart-parking platform designed to make finding parking in downtown Tuscaloosa easier. The concept combines a digital parking map, camera-based space detection, estimated parking turnover, and parking-demand trends.

How it could work

  1. Cameras view designated parking spaces.
  2. Computer vision detects whether each space is occupied.
  3. Availability information is sent to a database.
  4. The website displays open spaces and estimated trends.
Class prototype: This website is an interactive concept demonstration. It is not connected to City of Tuscaloosa infrastructure, real cameras, or live parking availability. The parking layout and trend data are fictional examples.
